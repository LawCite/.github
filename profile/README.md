LawCite gives detectives one place to work a case. You open an investigation, link people to vehicles and addresses on a board, keep evidence hashed and logged from the moment it's uploaded, and let an AI agent run the open-source lookups that would otherwise eat an afternoon of browser tabs.

It's built by a working law enforcement officer, and every account is verified before it gets in.

**Investigations.** Case files that hold the whole job, not one report. Link-analysis boards, a case map, task lists built from investigative templates, and evidence with its own SHA-256 chain of custody. Export the lot as one report with a hash manifest.

**Intel.** Give it a photo, a phone number, an email, a username or a VIN. It finds where a photo was taken, runs forensics on the image, traces accounts across the web, decodes the vehicle, and files what it finds back into the investigation.

**Case law.** Describe a situation and get the cases and statutes that apply in your state, with citations drawn only from LawCite's own database, so none of them are made up.
